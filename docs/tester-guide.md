# AI Help Desk Beta Tester Guide

Thank you for helping test AI Help Desk. The goal is simple: use it the way you would use a helpful first-level technology support person.

## Getting access

Access is by approval during the controlled beta. You will receive the tester URL and your access instructions after setup is complete.

## How to use it

1. Open the tester URL you were given.
2. Sign in or enter the authorized access information provided to you.
3. Describe your technology question or problem in ordinary words.
4. If AI Help Desk asks a question, answer what you actually see, hear, or experience.
5. Follow one suggested action at a time.
6. When asked whether the answer fixed the problem, choose **Yes** or **No**.

You do not need to know technical terminology. If you do not understand a question or instruction, say so.

## What to test

Real problems are especially useful: email, browsers, printers, Windows or Mac questions, phones/tablets, Zoom, Microsoft Office, passwords/access, and other ordinary technology issues. The product is still a beta, so some areas will work better than others.

## Safety and privacy

Do **not** enter passwords, one-time authentication/security codes, credit-card/bank information, Social Security numbers, or other highly sensitive information into the Help Desk.

Do not follow an instruction that asks you to send money, disclose a security code, weaken an important security setting, or do something that seems unsafe. Report it instead.

Testing activity and limited diagnostic/usage information may be recorded to improve the product. The beta should collect only information useful for troubleshooting and product improvement.

## Reporting a Help Desk problem

If the Help Desk itself behaves incorrectly, contact Tony. Please include:

- a short description of what you were trying to do
- what went wrong
- the case/session ID, when one is displayed
- a screenshot if it would help explain the problem

You are testing the product, not taking an exam. There are no wrong technology questions.
